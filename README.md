# Tiny Farm, Big Moon

A cute one-screen farm game for phones (portrait, one thumb). Tap to plant, swipe a whole row to harvest, and chain crops for Bumper and MEGA BUMPER bonuses. A giant, friendly moon watches from the sky.

**These are test builds.** It's not finished.

- **Stage 5 (newest):** a small fix to Stage 4. The animals on the hill are spaced out in up to two rows and shrink a little as the hill fills up, so they never overlap, even with all of them. Stage 4 saves carry over.
- **Stage 4:** polish and extras. A Settings screen (music and sound volume, Tap Harvest, Big Text, Reduce Motion, No Flashing, a color-blind palette, a season timer switch and Reset Progress), 15 achievements with an Album wall, a fairer late-game economy, a readable KEEP OUT sign, a moon that rises in front of the night sky, and many small fixes. Stage 3 saves carry over.
- **Stage 3:** the moon story. Build a rocket part by part over 5 Moon Years (coins, Moon Dust and Pip's wish lists), Pip's typed-out notes and an away note, the Daily Moon Gift, 10 hats for Pip, bigger animals on the hill, a fairer economy with real things to spend on, and the ending, credits and Zen mode. Stage 2 saves carry over.
- **Stage 2:** the full farm. Four 3-minute seasons and Moon Nights with Moon Dust, all 32 crops (plus golden versions from 3-in-a-row merges), crows and weeds, 12 animals with goods and special jobs, the upgrade shop with fields up to 6x6, crops that grow while you're away, saving and a Save Code, and the Farm Album.
- **Stage 1:** one 3-minute spring season on a 3x3 field (upgradable to 4x4), 6 spring crops, Pip the chick, swipe chains, Bumper and MEGA BUMPER, and the Farm Stand with 3 upgrades.

## Running locally

Serve the folder with any static web server, for example `python3 -m http.server`, then open `/stage5/`, `/stage4/`, `/stage3/`, `/stage2/` or `/stage1/`.
